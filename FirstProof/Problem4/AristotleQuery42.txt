2. W = tx + (1-t)y = weighted average
3. Z - W = t(1-t)(1/6 - (x+y)) < 0 (since x+y > 1/6)

Since t < 1/2, the weight on f_aux(y) is larger ((1-t) > 1/2).
And y < 1/12 means f_aux(y) is also in the increasing region.
While x > 1/12 means f_aux(x) could be in the decreasing region.
