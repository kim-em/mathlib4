FINITE FREE STAM INEQUALITY — Score identity and contraction via heat flow

=== GOAL ===

Fill the single sorry: finite_free_stam_inequality for ALL n ≥ 2.

=== STRATEGY: SCORE IDENTITY + CONTRACTION ===

Reference: Garza-Vargas, Srivastava, Stier (arXiv:2602.15822), Lemma 3.2 + §4.1.

The proof has three key ingredients:

1. SCORE IDENTITY (Lemma 3.2):
   (a+b) · J(γ) = D_Ω(a·J(α) ⊕ b·J(β))
   where γ = roots(p⊞q), α = roots(p), β = roots(q),
   J(α)ᵢ = Σ_{j≠i} 1/(αᵢ-αⱼ) is the score vector,
   and D_Ω is the Jacobian of the root map.

2. CONTRACTION (from variance identity + positivity):
   ||D_Ω(w)||² ≤ ||w||² for w orthogonal to (1,...,1,1,...,1)

3. COMBINING: From 1 + 2:
   (a+b)²||J(γ)||² ≤ a²||J(α)||² + b²||J(β)||²
   i.e., (a+b)²·Phi(p⊞q) ≤ a²·Phi(p) + b²·Phi(q)

=== SCORE IDENTITY PROOF SKETCH ===

The score identity is proved via heat flow dynamics:

For t ≥ 0, define heat flow: p_t(x) = e^{t/2 · ∂²ₓ} p(x).
This preserves real-rootedness.

Key fact: if α(t) are ordered roots of p_t, then α'(0) = J(α)
(root velocity equals score vector; this is Lemma 2.5).

Heat flow commutes with boxplus:
  (p ⊞_n q)_t = p_{at} ⊞_n q_{bt}  when t scales as (a+b)t

More precisely: (p ⊞_n q)_{(a+b)t} = p_{at} ⊞_n q_{bt}

Therefore: γ((a+b)t) = Ω(α(at), β(bt))

Differentiating at t=0:
  (a+b)·γ'(0) = D_Ω(a·α'(0), b·β'(0))
  (a+b)·J(γ) = D_Ω(a·J(α), b·J(β))

/-- Heat flow operator on polynomials. -/
noncomputable def heat_flow (t : ℝ) (p : ℝ[X]) : ℝ[X] :=
  ∑ k ∈ Finset.range (p.natDegree + 1),
    C ((t / 2) ^ k / k.factorial) * Polynomial.derivative^[2*k] p

/-- Heat flow commutes with boxplus (key commutativity). -/
lemma heat_flow_boxplus_comm (n : ℕ) (a b t : ℝ) (p q : ℝ[X])
    (hp_deg : p.natDegree = n) (hq_deg : q.natDegree = n) :
    heat_flow ((a + b) * t) (boxplus n p q) =
    boxplus n (heat_flow (a * t) p) (heat_flow (b * t) q) := by
  sorry

/-- The Phi contraction inequality.
    This combines the score identity, variance identity, and positivity. -/
lemma phi_contraction (n : ℕ) (hn : n ≥ 2) (a b : ℝ)

=== HEAT FLOW COMMUTATIVITY ===

The commutativity e^{(a+b)t/2·∂²}(p⊞q) = (e^{at/2·∂²}p) ⊞ (e^{bt/2·∂²}q)
follows from:
  boxplus n p q = p̂(∂)q  where p̂ is the "hat transform"
  e^{t∂²/2} commutes with ∂
  Therefore e^{t∂²/2}(p̂(∂)q) = p̂(∂)(e^{t∂²/2}q)

In terms of the hat polynomial: e^{t∂²/2}p = p ⊞ H_n(√t·x)
where H_n is the Hermite polynomial.
