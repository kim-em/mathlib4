FINITE FREE STAM INEQUALITY — General n proof with enriched context

=== GOAL ===

Prove the single sorry: finite_free_stam_inequality for all n ≥ 2.
  invPhi (boxplus n p q) ≥ invPhi p + invPhi q

The context file has 1095 lines of proven results including:

=== PROOF STRATEGY ===

STEP 1: CASE SPLIT on n.
  - n = 0, 1: impossible since hn : n ≥ 2.
  - n = 2: use finite_free_stam_inequality_n2 directly.
  - n ≥ 3: need the argument below.

Use `interval_cases` or `rcases n with _ | _ | _ | n` then `omega` for impossible cases.

STEP 2: CENTERING REDUCTION (for n ≥ 3).
By exists_shift_centered, find c, d such that p.comp(X+Cc) and q.comp(X+Cd) are centered.
By invPhi_comp_add_C: invPhi is unchanged by the shift.
By boxplus_comp_add_C: boxplus(p.comp(X+Cc), q.comp(X+Cd)) = boxplus(p,q).comp(X+C(c+d)).
So WLOG p and q are centered (coeff(n-1) = 0).

STEP 3: EXPRESS invPhi IN TERMS OF ROOTS.
invPhi(p) = 1/Phi(p) when p has distinct roots and degree ≥ 2.
Phi(p) = Σ_i S_i² where S_i = Σ_{j≠i} 1/(λ_i - λ_j).

KEY IDENTITY: Σ_i S_i = 0 (antisymmetry: pair (i,j) contributes 1/(λ_i-λ_j) + 1/(λ_j-λ_i) = 0).

For the convolution p ⊞_n q with roots μ_1,...,μ_n:
We need: 1/Σ S_k(p⊞q)² ≥ 1/Σ S_i(p)² + 1/Σ S_j(q)²

Equivalently: Σ S_i(p)² · Σ S_j(q)² ≥ Σ S_k(p⊞q)² · (Σ S_i(p)² + Σ S_j(q)²)

This is the HARMONIC MEAN INEQUALITY for Phi:
  Phi(p⊞q) ≤ Phi(p)·Phi(q) / (Phi(p) + Phi(q))

STEP 4: THE MISSING LINK — connecting roots of p⊞q to roots of p and q.

The context proves cumulant additivity (ff_kappa_additive), but there is NO
direct formula expressing Phi in terms of cumulants for general n.
This is the KEY GAP.

POSSIBLE APPROACHES TO BRIDGE THIS GAP:

(A) DERIVE Phi IN TERMS OF COEFFICIENTS for general n.
    For centered degree-n poly with roots λ_1,...,λ_n (sum = 0):
    The power sums p_k = Σ λ_i^k relate to coefficients via Newton's identities.
    And Phi = Σ S_i² = Σ_i (Σ_{j≠i} 1/(λ_i-λ_j))² is related to the discriminant:
      disc(p) = Π_{i<j} (λ_i - λ_j)²
    By Phi_derivative_form, Phi = Σ (p''(λ_i)/(2p'(λ_i)))².
    Since p'(λ_i) = Π_{j≠i}(λ_i-λ_j), we get Phi·disc = Σ [p''(λ_i)/2]² · disc/[p'(λ_i)]².
    This equals Σ [p''(λ_i)/2]² · Π_{j≠i, k<l, (k,l)∩{i}=∅} (λ_k-λ_l)².

(B) USE THE n=3 PATTERN DIRECTLY for general n.
    For n=3: invPhi = -(2/9)a - (3/2)(b/a)² = linear(κ₂) + Cauchy-Schwarz-superadditive(κ₃/κ₂).
    Conjecture for general n: invPhi_n = c_n·κ₂ + R(κ₂,...,κ_n) where R is superadditive.
    The linear part c_n·κ₂ is superadditive by cumulant additivity.
    Prove R is superadditive by showing it has the form Σ(numerator²/denominator)
    where the denominator is itself superadditive.

(C) PROVE Phi IS CONCAVE in roots (in an appropriate sense), then use:
    Phi(expected roots) ≤ expected Phi, giving the bound.
