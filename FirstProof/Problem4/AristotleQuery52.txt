=== n=3 PROOF OUTLINE ===

For centered cubics p = X³ + aX + b:
- boxplus(3, p₁, p₂) = X³ + (a₁+a₂)X + (b₁+b₂) [coefficients add for centered]
- invPhi(p) = invPhi_cubic_centered_formula(a, b) = -(4a³ + 27b²)/(18a²)
- Need: -(4(a₁+a₂)³ + 27(b₁+b₂)²)/(18(a₁+a₂)²) ≥ -(4a₁³+27b₁²)/(18a₁²) + -(4a₂³+27b₂²)/(18a₂²)
- The a-cubed terms cancel perfectly
- Reduces to: a₁²a₂²(b₁+b₂)² ≤ (a₁+a₂)²(a₂²b₁² + a₁²b₂²)
- This is: (a₁²b₂ - a₂²b₁)² ≥ 0 after expansion (plus positive correction from a₁a₂ > 0)

=== n=4 SYMMETRIC PROOF OUTLINE ===
